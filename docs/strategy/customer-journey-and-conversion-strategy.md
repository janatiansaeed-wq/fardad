# Customer Journey & Conversion Strategy

## Document Information

- Project: Fardad Platform
- Type: Customer Experience & Conversion Strategy
- Purpose: Define customer journey, purchasing psychology, conversion optimization, and ethical sales improvement
- Status: Strategic Foundation Document


# 1. Vision

Fardad Platform should create a customer experience where users feel:

- respected
- informed
- empowered
- in control

The system should not force customers to buy.

The system should help customers make better decisions.

The goal:


Customer Freedom

Smart Assistance

Better Experience

=

Higher Conversion



# 2. Core Philosophy

## Customer Is The Decision Maker

Customers have different behaviors:

Some customers:

- know exactly what they want
- search directly for a product
- dislike unnecessary questions

Other customers:

- need guidance
- compare options
- need confidence before purchase


The platform must support both.


# 3. Customer Choice Principle

Never block the customer journey.

Bad experience:


Please answer these questions before viewing products.


Better experience:


Need help choosing?

We can suggest suitable options.


Provide:


[Choose Myself]

[Help Me Choose]


The customer always remains in control.


# 4. Customer Journey Model


## Stage 1 — Discovery

Customer enters through:

- Google search
- Social media
- Direct visit
- Digital catalog
- Campaign pages


System objectives:

- Build trust
- Explain value
- Reduce uncertainty


Important elements:

- High quality images
- Product story
- Brand credibility
- Clear navigation


---

# Stage 2 — Exploration

Customer views:

- Categories
- Products
- Articles
- Collections


System should provide:

- Relevant information
- Comparison capability
- Related content


Avoid:

- Excessive popups
- Too many recommendations
- Confusing navigation


---

# Stage 3 — Product Evaluation


Product page should include:


## Product Information

- Images
- Lifestyle images
- Video when available
- Description
- Specifications
- Story
- Dimensions
- Weight


## Trust Elements

- Authenticity information
- Production story
- Packaging information
- Customer reviews


---

# 5. Smart Guidance System

Guidance must be optional.

Examples:


## Gift Assistant

Instead of:

"What are you buying for?"

Use:

"Looking for a gift? We can help you find a suitable option."


Possible inputs:

- Occasion
- Budget
- Style preference
- Recipient type


The customer may skip anytime.


---

# 6. Product Recommendation Strategy


Recommendations should appear based on context.


## Type 1 — Similar Products

Purpose:

Help comparison.

Example:

"Explore similar designs"


---

## Type 2 — Complete The Experience

Purpose:

Increase order value.


Example:

Product:

Handicraft artwork


Suggestion:

- Premium box
- Greeting card
- Gift wrapping


---

## Type 3 — Frequently Selected Together


Example:

"Customers who selected this product also selected:"


Rules:

Do not overload the customer.

Maximum recommendation areas should be limited.


---

# 7. Upselling Strategy


Upselling must provide value.


Bad:

"Buy a more expensive version."


Better:

"Upgrade your gift experience."


Examples:

Base:

Product only


Suggested:

Product

+

Premium packaging

+

Personal message


---

# 8. Cross-Selling Strategy


Cross-selling should feel natural.


Examples:


Product:

Copper artwork


Suggestions:

- Matching decoration
- Gift box
- Certificate


The recommendation must improve the customer's goal.


---

# 9. Product Configuration Experience


For configurable products:


Journey:


Select Product

↓

Choose Options

↓

Add Services

↓

Personalize

↓

Review Final Experience

↓

Purchase



The customer should always see:

- Selected options
- Price changes
- Final result


---

# 10. Gamification Principles


Use progress indicators carefully.


Examples:


"Your gift is 80% complete."


"Only one step remains."


Purpose:

- Increase engagement
- Reduce abandonment


Avoid:

- Artificial pressure
- Manipulative countdowns


---

# 11. Cart Experience


Cart should help completion.


Features:

- Selected items summary
- Packaging summary
- Services summary
- Delivery information


Suggestions:

Only relevant recommendations.


---

# 12. Abandoned Journey Recovery


Not every visitor should receive messages.


Priority:


High intent:

- Added to cart
- Started checkout
- Selected customization


Low intent:

- Single page visit


Recovery methods:

- Reminder
- Helpful content
- Product information


Avoid aggressive messaging.


---

# 13. Customer Retention Strategy


After purchase:

The relationship continues.


System can support:

- Purchase history
- Personalized recommendations
- Occasion reminders
- Repeat purchase campaigns


Examples:

Corporate customer:

"Your previous Nowruz gift order can be renewed."


---

Corporate customers require a different experience.


Journey:


Discover

↓

Request Consultation

↓

Select Products

↓

Customize

↓

Approve

↓

Bulk Order

↓

Repeat Purchase



Required capabilities:

- Corporate inquiry
- Custom packaging
- Logo services
- Formal quotation


---

# 15. Analytics Integration


Every journey stage should generate anonymous insights.


Examples:


Discovery:

- Source
- Landing page


Exploration:

- Categories viewed
- Search behavior


Evaluation:

- Product views
- Configuration actions


Purchase:

- Conversion
- Order value


---

# 16. Cultural Adaptation


The platform should consider local customer behavior.


Principles:

- Respect customer expertise
- Avoid excessive questioning
- Provide assistance when needed
- Build trust before selling

# 18. Conversion Optimization Rules


The platform should continuously improve:

- Product pages
- Navigation
- Recommendations
- Checkout experience


Based on:

- Analytics
- Testing
- Customer feedback


---

# Final Principle


The best commerce experience is not forcing customers to buy.

It is creating an environment where customers confidently choose the best solution.

Fardad Platform should make customers feel:

"I found exactly what I needed."

While the system continuously learns how to serve them better.
