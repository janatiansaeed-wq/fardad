# Marketing Intelligence Assistant Vision

## Document Information

- Project: Fardad Platform
- Type: Strategic Intelligence Architecture
- Purpose: Define marketing intelligence, campaign assistance, and business growth assistant
- Status: Strategic Foundation Document


# 1. Vision

Fardad Platform should evolve from a website management system into a business growth intelligence platform.

The platform should help managers answer:

- What should we sell?
- When should we promote it?
- Which customers should we target?
- Which opportunities are being missed?
- What actions should we take next?


The goal:


Data

Behavior Understanding

Business Knowledge

Action Recommendations

=

Marketing Intelligence



# 2. Core Philosophy

The system should not replace the manager.

The system should become a decision-support assistant.

It should:

- Detect opportunities
- Identify risks
- Recommend actions
- Explain business trends


Final decisions remain with humans.

# 4. Event Preparation Assistant

The system should provide reminders.

Example:

Recommended preparation:

□ Select products
□ Prepare landing page
□ Create campaign banner
□ Prepare content
□ Review customer segments



# 5. Customer Intelligence Layer


The system should analyze anonymous customer behavior.


Examples:

- Viewed products
- Search behavior
- Categories visited
- Shopping cart actions
- Purchase history
- Product preferences


The system should create segments.


Examples:


Customers interested in luxury gifts

Customers interested in corporate gifts

Customers who abandoned checkout

Returning customers

# 8. Customer Journey Monitoring


The system should identify important behaviors.


Examples:


High Intent User:


Viewed product multiple times

Added to cart

Started checkout



Possible action:


Consider reminder campaign



Low Intent User:


Single page visit



No aggressive action.

The system should identify:

- Abandoned carts
- Abandoned configurations
- Unfinished inquiries

but did not complete purchase.

Review packaging pricing or explanation.

# 14. Ethical Intelligence Principles


The system must:

- Respect customer privacy
- Avoid manipulation
- Provide useful recommendations
- Support transparent marketing


The objective:

Better customer experience.

Not customer exploitation.
