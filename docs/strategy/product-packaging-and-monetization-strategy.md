# Product Packaging & Monetization Strategy

## Document Information

- Project: Fardad Platform
- Type: Product Strategy Document
- Purpose: Define software packaging, monetization model, and module activation strategy
- Status: Strategic Foundation Document


# 1. Vision

Fardad Platform should not be delivered as a single fixed website.

It should operate as a modular digital commerce platform.

Customers should be able to start with a professional foundation and upgrade based on their business growth.

The platform follows:


Start Simple

↓

Grow With Business

↓

Activate Advanced Intelligence

↓

Become A Digital Business Platform



# 2. Business Model Principle

The platform is based on:

- Modular architecture
- Feature activation
- Tier-based packages
- Optional premium capabilities

The goal:

Create long-term customer relationships instead of one-time website projects.


# 3. Product Tiers Overview

The platform has four main packages:
